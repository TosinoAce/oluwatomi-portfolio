Leadership

People have trusted me to speak for them since secondary school. Here's where, and what I did with that trust.

2024 – PresentUniversity of Ilorin

Female Course Representative, Mavens' Class of '30

Picture more than 200 medical students who all need the same information at the same time. I'm one of the links between them and our lecturers. I pass on what matters, keep everyone in the loop, and make sure my classmates' concerns reach the people who can act on them.

Beyond the everyday work, I helped bring two class projects to life.

May 2025, planning committee

Neglected Tropical Diseases Outreach Programme

We took health education to pupils at Unilorin Primary School and taught them about neglected tropical diseases and how to stay safe from them.

July 2025, logistics

Mavens Hangout 1.0

After a long 100 level, our class deserved a day off together. I helped plan it and coordinate the arrangements that made it happen.

2022 – 2023Watered Garden International School

Teacher

Before I studied how to care for patients, I learned how to explain things to young children.

  • I taught a Basic 4 class and helped my pupils understand their classroom subjects.
  • I also gave private home lessons to two younger children, one in Basic 1 and one in Nursery 1.
2017 – 20182020 – 2021Ikotun Junior and Senior Secondary School

Head Girl, twice

I was Head Girl in junior school and again in senior school, working closely with fellow students, prefects, teachers and school authorities.

  • We threw the first valedictory our school had ever had. As a government school, we had never held a valedictory service or graduation celebration. So the other prefects and I decided to organise one ourselves for SS3, with our teachers and school authorities behind us. I also gave a speech on the day.
  • We found our own sponsors. We wrote letters to businesses around the school asking for support. A local restaurant supplied our cake, and Twinsfaja Supermarket made a generous donation.
